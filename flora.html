The Flora experience

Come for dinner. Stay for the feeling.

Flora is our intimate dining room on Las Olas Boulevard, built around the simple pleasure of eating beautifully and lingering a little longer. Our kitchen turns seasonal ingredients into hand-pulled pasta, generous plates, and rituals worth returning for.
