Our Story

From a single candle on Las Olas to a family of restaurants — this is La Felicita.

La Felicità Group began with a simple belief: that the most memorable meals happen when the room is warm, the light is low, and the conversation flows like wine.

What started as Flora — a small, private dining room on Las Olas Boulevard — grew into a collection of distinct Italian experiences across Fort Lauderdale. Each venue carries the same DNA: hand-pulled pasta, curated Italian wine, and candlelight that makes every guest feel like the only one in the room.

We are independent, family-driven, and deeply connected to the local community. Our chefs, sommeliers, and hospitality teams are the heart of everything we do.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

✧ Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?
Absolutely. Our chefs can accommodate gluten-free, vegetarian, vegan, and most allergies with advance notice. Please note any restrictions in your reservation.
✧ Is there a dress code?
We suggest smart casual. Many guests enjoy dressing up for the candlelit experience — it's part of the ritual.
✧ Do you offer private dining or buyouts?
Yes — full venue buyouts and semi-private rooms are available for 8–60 guests. Visit our Private Events section or contact us directly.
✧ Can I arrive by water taxi?
Yes! We partner with the Fort Lauderdale water taxi for dockside drop-offs at select venues. Book the "water taxi arrival package" when reserving.
✧ Are gift cards redeemable at all venues?
Yes — all La Felicità Group gift cards are valid at any of our restaurants, including Flora, Giardino, Limone, and Enotecha.
